# Part 2 – Computer Disassembly
## Before Opening the Computer
Describe the computer before opening it.

The system was a desktop tower in good cosmetic condition: metal and plastic case, front panel with power button, USB ports, and ventilation grilles. Cables connected the monitor, keyboard, mouse, and mains power. From the outside, the main visible elements were the case, optical drive bay (unused on this unit), and rear I/O shield with video, USB, Ethernet, and audio jacks. The machine was powered off and unplugged before any work began, and the workspace was clear of static-sensitive hazards.

## Internal Layout
Open the computer case and observe the internal components.
Describe what you see.

Inside the case, the motherboard was mounted on standoffs with the CPU and heatsink assembly near the centre-top. RAM modules sat in adjacent slots; a SATA SSD and a hard drive were fixed in drive bays with data and power cables routed to the motherboard. The power supply unit occupied the bottom-rear corner with bundled cables feeding the board and drives. A case fan drew air across the CPU heatsink and out through the rear. Cables were generally tidy, with the 24-pin ATX, CPU power, and SATA connectors clearly visible.
